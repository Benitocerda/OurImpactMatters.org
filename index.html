Our Impact Matters

We are a dedicated nonprofit organization with a mission to support underserved communities in need. Founded by Dr. Oquendo, Our Impact Matters began with the vision of creating a more balanced Chicago—where every neighborhood has access to resources, support, and opportunity.

Our Mission

We are a dedicated nonprofit organization committed to uplifting underserved communities by providing access to resources, support, and opportunity. Our goal is to create lasting impact through compassion, action, and unity.
